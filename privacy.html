Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

What the tool accesses. After you approve access on TikTok's login screen, the tool receives an access token and, through the user.info.basic scope, basic profile information. It uses the video.upload scope only to send the video file you choose to your own TikTok account.

Where data is stored. Access tokens and settings are stored only in files on your own computer. The developer runs no server and does not receive, collect, store, sell or share your personal data, tokens or videos.

Third parties. Videos are sent directly from your computer to the platform you select (TikTok, YouTube or Instagram). Their own privacy policies apply to data they receive.

Your control. You can delete the stored token file at any time and revoke the tool's access in your TikTok account settings (Security and permissions, then Apps and services permissions).

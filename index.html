pozny

pozny is a small desktop tool that lets its owner upload their own videos to their own YouTube, TikTok and Instagram accounts with one click.

It runs only on the owner's computer. It connects to TikTok through the official Login Kit and Content Posting API, and only after the account owner approves access. Videos are uploaded from the owner's disk straight to the platform as drafts or posts on that same account.
